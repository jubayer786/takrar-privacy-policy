Option 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the Takrar app installed on your Android device:

  • Open the Takrar app.
  • Go to the Profile tab.
  • Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm your request in the dialog.

Your student profile, learning streak, exam history, and progress records will be instantly erased from our live databases in real-time.

Option 2: Submit a Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can request full account deletion by emailing our support team:
