If you choose to use our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. 1. Information Collection and Use

To provide an enhanced, personalized educational experience, calculate your academic progress, and securely sync your learning data across devices, we collect information across the following categories:

A. Authentication & Account Data (via Google Sign-In & Firebase Auth):

When you sign in using Google or Firebase Authentication, we collect:

  • Google Account Name & Email Address: Used to uniquely register and authenticate your student account and provide customer support.
  • Profile Picture / Photo URL: Retrieved from your authenticated Google account to set your initial student profile picture and avatar within the app. (Categorized under Personal Info in Google Play Data Safety as an account display element; Takrar does not access device photo galleries or request camera permissions).
  • Firebase User Identifier (UID) & Auth Tokens: A unique cryptographic user identifier generated by Firebase Authentication to securely link your database records, learning progress, and exam history to your account.

B. Student Profile & Onboarding Information (Directly Provided by User):

  • Custom Display Name: Provided during profile setup to be displayed on your student profile card, streak count, and academic leaderboards (allowing you to use a preferred student name separate from your Google account).
  • Country: Selected during onboarding to personalize your educational experience, adapt interface language settings, deliver localized study reminders in your preferred language, and display your national flag on academic leaderboards to foster friendly peer motivation and global student engagement.
  • Academic Details (Institution, Curriculum & Academic Stage): Collected to structure syllabus chapters, provide appropriate study materials and question banks, and organize personalized study routines based on the user's selected educational program (encompassing classical Arabic linguistic studies, prerequisite academic disciplines, and higher university faculty specializations).

Purpose of Collection and Sharing for Google Play Transparency:

  • Collected (Account & Educational Functionality — Not Shared with Ad Networks):
    • Student Profile & Authentication: Name, email, student avatar, UID, and academic study level. Collected strictly for authentication, personalized revision routines, and leaderboard management. Never sold or shared with third parties or data brokers.
    • Diagnostics & API Performance: Network latency, API request speed and duration, and crash stack traces (via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring). Collected exclusively on our behalf to resolve bugs and optimize network and server speed.
  • Shared with Third Parties (Advertising & Marketing Attribution):
    • Technical & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), App Set ID, device model, operating system version, and approximate IP location are shared with Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent ad fraud.
    • App Activity Milestones: High-level app milestone events (e.g., registration completed, practice session started) are shared with Meta (Facebook SDK) for campaign measurement and aggregated engagement analytics. No personal student credentials, passwords, or quiz answers are ever shared with Meta.

C. Technical, Device & Location Identifiers:

  • Device & Technical Identifiers: Unique device identifiers, push notification tokens (FCM), operating system version, device platform/model, app version, interface language/locale, IP address, Android Advertising ID (GAID), and App Set ID.
  • Automatic Ad Network Collection (Google Mobile Ads SDK & Meta): These networks may automatically collect and share certain technical, diagnostic, and usage data for advertising, analytics, frequency capping, and fraud prevention purposes in accordance with Google's Play Data Disclosure standards, including:
    • IP Address: Automatically processed for fraud/abuse prevention, network diagnostics, and approximate geographic routing.
    • User Product Interactions: User ad interactions (such as ad impressions, taps, clicks, and video completion events).
    • Diagnostic Information: SDK-level performance diagnostics, ad loading latency, and crash/error reports.
    • Device or Other Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (GAID), App Set ID, and applicable platform identifiers.
  • Location Information (Approximate vs. Precise):
    • No Device Location Permissions: Takrar does not request or collect device location through Android location permissions.
    • Approximate Location (IP-Derived): Integrated third-party SDKs (including Google Mobile Ads and Meta) may process IP address and derive approximate geographic information for advertising, fraud prevention, campaign attribution, or related purposes. Where required by Google Play's SDK-specific Data Safety guidance, such processing is disclosed as Approximate Location.

2. System Permissions and Declarations

All system permissions utilized by Takrar serve transparent and necessary application functions:

  • Internet Access & Network State Detection: Required to communicate with our secure backend servers, verify connectivity, and seamlessly switch between live syncing and local offline study modes.
  • Push Notifications: Used on Android 13+ to deliver daily study schedule reminders, streak alerts, and exam notices. A push notification token (FCM) is processed to deliver these reminders to your device; the token is invalidated by Firebase upon uninstallation or rotation, and is purged from our backend device registries upon user logout, account deletion, or when identified as stale/unregistered during delivery attempts. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings.
  • Google Advertising ID & Platform Ad Identifiers: Utilized by certified ad networks (Google Mobile Ads & Meta) to prevent ad fraud, limit ad frequency, and deliver privacy-preserving contextual ads in accordance with modern Android advertising standards. (Takrar does not collect, query, or process Topics or AdServices data directly; modern Android Privacy Sandbox and AdServices capabilities on supported devices such as Android 13+ are managed independently and automatically by certified SDK mediation, including Google Mobile Ads, in accordance with Google Play developer standards).
  • Wake State Management (WAKE_LOCK): Used strictly by Firebase Cloud Messaging and audio synthesis handlers to ensure reliable push notification delivery and uninterrupted educational audio playback.

Sensitive Permissions We Never Use:

Takrar does not request or access your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phonebook, SMS, or Precise GPS Location.

4. Audio and Educational Features

We utilize high-quality Text-to-Speech (TTS) synthesis powered by Microsoft Edge Speech Services and local audio players strictly for reciting educational Arabic lessons, reading passages, and quiz questions with accurate pronunciation.

  • No Voice Recording: The application does not record, access, collect, or transmit your voice or microphone audio under any circumstance.
  • Real-Time Text Processing Only: Only standardized public educational Arabic text from our curriculum is transmitted to the TTS engine to generate transient audio playback, which is then cached locally on your device to minimize bandwidth. Takrar does not transmit any student personal information, user-generated text, or voice recordings to the TTS service. Third-party speech synthesis is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

To maintain application reliability, optimize API response speed, and eliminate crashes:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects diagnostic stack traces, device hardware model, operating system version, and application state at the time of an error.

This technical data is utilized strictly for system diagnostics, server performance tuning, and software stability.

  • Remember your selected interface language and local application preferences.
